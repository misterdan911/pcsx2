I.	Introduction

The strangely dressed girl knocks on Rikido Hiroki窶冱 doorstep 
as she smiles at him innocently. 
"I'm a private nurse, and I am going to cure you!"
Hiroki has always suffered from a disease which weakens him. 
He does no more than go to school and come home, 
accompanied by his childhood friend Ayano, 
who loves to dote over him. 
He lives alone, quietly suffering and enduring his disease. 

But all of this changes with the arrival of Maria, 
who is a 窶弃rivate Nurse窶 hired by Hiroki's mother. 
Nurse Maria forces her way into Hiroki's sheltered existence, 
promising to cure him for good. 
Hiroki tries to make Maria leave because 
he doesn't want to lose his carefree life. 
However, no matter how hard he tries, she just won't leave.
 
Hiroki decides to give her a one-week trial period. 
If his condition improves within a week, 
he'll let her stay with him for the whole month. 
Unsure of what to make of this beautiful new intruder, 
Ayano watches with conflicting emotions from the side-lines.
Maria happily accepts his offer.
Ayano silently watches Hiroki with mixed feelings.
This is the beginning of a new lifestyle.


II.	Production Information

History: The original game was released by Angel Smile 
in Japan in 2001. 
The game was successful and was translated into English 
and subsequently released by G-Collections in 2002. 
The game was also released in Russian by Macho Studio and Triada.
A PS2 port was released by DATAM Polystar in 2003 featuring 
additional content.

I personally hope to see the characters again someday... 
in a new setup :)

IV.	Cast

Rikido Hiroki: Hiroki has been sick all through his life. 
Nobody knows the exact reason for his strange sickness, 
though it seems to be as psychological as it is physical. 
His life starts to change when Maria visits him 
and promises to cure him in 30 days. 
He is childhood friends with Ayano.

Maria (Voiced by Emi Honi (Emi Motoi)): A legendary 
"Private Nurse," reputed to be the best in the world, 
above and beyond any practitioner in hospitals. 
She is always smiling, and has an uncanny ability 
to be kind and gentle while always getting her way. 
She loves her work, but there is always time for a little TV!

Miyamori Ayano (Voiced by Satomi Koorogi): Hiroki's 
childhood friend. They have lived next to each other forever 
and she is the only one who understands him. 
She's an intelligent, athletic girl who treats 
Hiroki as a person, not a sick boy.
However, she is extremely tomboyish and has no patience. 
Maria's arrival unsettles Ayano 
and makes her re-examine her feelings. 

Sasho Mio/ Aso Miharu (Voiced by Mioko Fujiwara): 
The nurse at Hiroki's school. 
He has gotten to know her because he spends 
a lot of time there due to his illness. 
She is always cold to everyone, 
and seems to do nothing but sleep while she's at work. 
It seems the only way to crack her shell is 
to offer her something sweet to eat! 

Yoshino and Yuno: Ladies of the Miyamori family. 
Yoshino is the Mother and Yuko is the daughter.
Both to see Ayano and Hiroki together. 
Yuno is like her mother in her attitude.

Perapera and Itome: 窶弋win Giant Private Nurses窶?

Perapera has a habit of talking very fast while 
Itome seems to be sleeping all the time. 
They are over 180 cm. tall.

Juliet (Voiced by Sayaka Aoki): Another 窶弃rivate Nurse窶? 
and the only colleague and friend of Maria. 

Rikido Hiromi: Hiroki窶冱 mother.

Nishinomori: Hiroki窶冱 Childhood friend

V.	Paths and Walkthrough

There are a total of 4 endings in the game, 
good endings for Maria, Ayano and Mio and one neutral ending. 
Once you view all endings,
you unlock a special scene at the end of Maria's route 
and Maria窶冱 face appears on the title screen.
Completing Maria窶冱 and Ayano窶冱 paths unlocks their 窶廣fter Diaries窶 
which have several short stories and give more information
on the story and on Maria.

During the 30-day period, Ayano will play Jan-Ken-Pon 
(Rock-Paper-Scissors) with you three times.
The outcome slightly impacts additional elements of 
Maria窶冱 and Ayano's story. I will detail more on this in their 
respective routes below. Ayano draws randomly, 
but it seems that the chances of her drawing rock
first are high. I would also suggest you save before each game.

The story is set in a span of 30 days 
with 15-20 choices in each of the routes. 
Also, while you can save at any point, 
the game loads from the beginning of the day.
